# Deal lead's assessment lens (calibration context)

Captured from the PE team's feedback on prior ADCG memos. Use it to calibrate scoring and the Pros/Cons voice.

- The Apave and The Compleat Food Group IC memos were rated great — they validate the memo structure, the
  sector-tailored scorecard and the calibration (Apave ≈ 6.16 / risk 38; Compleat ≈ 5.4 / risk 46).
- Underwrite on HARD / agency-defined EBITDA, not company "adjusted" EBITDA; treat the gap as a top diligence gate
  (Apave 10.6% vs 14.4%; Circet ~10.5% agency vs ~13% adjusted).
- Penalise leverage heavily once net debt > ~5x on thin-margin / capital-intensive assets (Compleat 4.9x, Circet ~6.0x
  both capped at 5.4 / risk 46); reward genuinely low leverage (Apave 2.9x).
- Right-comp discipline: anchor to the correct peer set, never a blended-sector average — services/contractors sit well
  below asset owners (Circet ~7–9x services vs 11–20x owners; Compleat vs Bakkavor take-out ~7.5–7.9x).
- Distrust M&A- or price-manufactured growth with thin organic growth — score Business Quality down.
- CV / sponsor-set-price situations carry a governance discount until a fairness opinion + (for a minority) governance
  rights exist.
- Every run STARTS with a Company & Industry Primer before scoring — genesis, management team, heavy emphasis on the
  business model and how value is captured, the full value chain with visuals and examples at each layer, 2–3 named
  customers (what they need), 4–5 named suppliers (terms, what is paid), unit economics and margins by layer, how
  competitors differ (pricing, customer type, ownership of key assets), entry threat from hyperscalers/incumbents and
  why strategic investors back the field, market sizing in the right units ($ vs GW vs volume), and a first-principles
  test of whether the model deserves its multiple — then the scoring, competitors and valuation.
